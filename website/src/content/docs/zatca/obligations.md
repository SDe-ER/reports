This page lists the main Saudi VAT obligations of a VAT-registered business. It also lists the
official rules that the [KSA VAT Return](/reports/reports/ksa-vat-return/) report is built on. Every statement
links to its source on the website of the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA). The Arabic versions of the
ZATCA guidelines prevail; the English versions are for guidance.

:::caution[Not legal advice]
This page summarises public ZATCA guidance as of November 2026. It is not legal or tax advice, and Sijill is not
certified by ZATCA. ZATCA updates its guidelines: check the linked document before you rely on a rule.
:::

## Registration

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Registration is mandatory when annual taxable supplies exceed SAR 375,000. | [ZATCA FAQ 024](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/FAQs/Pages/FAQ_024.aspx) |
| Voluntary registration is possible above SAR 187,500. | [ZATCA FAQ 024](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/FAQs/Pages/FAQ_024.aspx) |
| The registration service. | [ZATCA e-service: VAT registration](https://www.zatca.gov.sa/en/eServices/Pages/eServices_002.aspx) |

## Rate

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| The basic VAT rate is 15%. | [Guideline on the change of the basic VAT rate to 15%](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/VAT15.pdf) |

## Filing the return

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Businesses with annual supplies above SAR 40 million file monthly; others file quarterly. | [ZATCA news, Q2 2026 returns](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/News/Pages/VAT-Returns-submission-for-Q2-June-2026.aspx) |
| The return is due by the last day of the month after the period. Example: Q3 2026 by 31 October 2026. | [ZATCA news, Q3 2026 returns](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/News/Pages/Submit-VAT-Return-for-Oct-andQ3-2026.aspx) |
| Late filing: a fine of 5% to 25% of the tax that had to be declared. | [ZATCA news, Q2 2026 returns](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/News/Pages/VAT-Returns-submission-for-Q2-June-2026.aspx) |
| The form has three steps: sales, purchases and total VAT. | [User guide: Submit VAT Return](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/eServices/Documents/eServices_Manual_009.pdf) |
| Sales and purchases are entered excluding VAT; the portal calculates the VAT. | [Simplified VAT filing guide](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Simplified_VAT_Filing_Guidelines.pdf) |
| Only purchases with a valid tax invoice, related to the taxable activity, are entered as recoverable. | [Simplified VAT filing guide](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Simplified_VAT_Filing_Guidelines.pdf) |
| Do not use the sales adjustment field to reduce VAT without a supporting document. | [Simplified VAT filing guide](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Simplified_VAT_Filing_Guidelines.pdf) |
| Worked examples of complete returns. | [VAT return filing manual (Arabic)](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/VAT_Return_Filing_Manual_Arabic20190109.pdf) |

## Correcting a filed return

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| An error with a tax difference above SAR 15,000: notify ZATCA within 20 days of becoming aware of it, and amend the earlier return. | [Guideline: Tax Invoicing and Records, section 10.4](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-for-Tax-Invoicing-and-Records-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| A smaller error can be corrected in the return of the period in which it was found. The corrections field takes the VAT amount only. | [Simplified VAT filing guide](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Simplified_VAT_Filing_Guidelines.pdf) |
| An incorrect return that lowers the tax due: a fine of 50% of the difference. | [ZATCA VAT penalties](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/VAT/Pages/Penalties.aspx) |

## Tax invoices

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| A tax invoice must meet Article 53 of the VAT Implementing Regulations. | [ZATCA FAQ 006](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/FAQs/Pages/FAQ_006.aspx) |
| Required content: the title "Tax Invoice", dates, and a sequential number. It also carries the supplier's name, address and VAT number, and the customer's details (with the VAT number if registered). The supply lines show description, unit price, quantity, discount, total excluding VAT, rate, VAT amount and total including VAT. | [Guideline: Tax Invoicing and Records, section 4.2](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-for-Tax-Invoicing-and-Records-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| Invoices must be issued in Arabic (other languages may be added), in Saudi riyals. | [Guideline: Tax Invoicing and Records, section 4.2.1](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-for-Tax-Invoicing-and-Records-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| A tax invoice is issued no later than 15 days after the end of the month of the supply. | [Guideline: Tax Invoicing and Records, section 4.5](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-for-Tax-Invoicing-and-Records-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| Any document by which an amount of tax is collected is treated as a tax invoice (Article 44 of the VAT Law). | [Guideline: Tax Invoicing and Records, section 2](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-for-Tax-Invoicing-and-Records-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |

## Credit and debit notes

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| A note meets the tax invoice requirements of Article 53. It states its type and the number of the original invoice. | [Guideline: Tax Invoicing and Records, section 7](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-for-Tax-Invoicing-and-Records-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| A note is issued within 15 days of the month following the event that requires it. | [Guideline: Tax Invoicing and Records, section 7.2](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-for-Tax-Invoicing-and-Records-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |

## E-invoicing (FATOORA)

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| E-invoices are generated in a structured format by a compliant solution and stored, with a QR code. | [E-invoicing simplified guide](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/E-Invoicing/Introduction/Guidelines/Documents/Simplified%20guide_En.pdf) |
| Phase 2 (integration) connects the solution to FATOORA. It is rolled out in waves; ZATCA notifies each wave at least six months ahead. | [ZATCA news, Wave 25](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/News/Pages/Wave25-E-invoicing.aspx) |
| To deduct input tax, the e-invoices and notes must be cleared by or reported to ZATCA as the integration phase requires. | [E-invoicing implementation resolution](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/E-Invoicing/Introduction/LawsAndRegulations/Documents/20230519_E-Invoicing%20Implementation%20Resolution%20English.pdf) |
| The e-invoicing regulation. | [E-invoicing regulation](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/E-Invoicing/Introduction/LawsAndRegulations/Documents/E-invoicing-Regulations.pdf) |

## Input VAT deduction

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Input VAT is deductible when it relates to the economic activity and was correctly charged by a KSA-registered supplier. | [Guideline on Input Tax Deduction, sections 4 and 5](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-on-Input-Tax-Deduction-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| The business must hold a valid tax invoice. Alternative evidence is accepted only at ZATCA's discretion. | [Guideline on Input Tax Deduction, section 8](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-on-Input-Tax-Deduction-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| Input VAT on private use, on restricted categories, and on exempt supplies is not deductible. | [Guideline on Input Tax Deduction, sections 7 and 12](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-on-Input-Tax-Deduction-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| A deduction not claimed in its period can be claimed later, within five years. | [Guideline on Input Tax Deduction, section 9](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-on-Input-Tax-Deduction-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| Bank fees charged as an explicit fee or commission are taxable at 15%. Implicit margins and interest are generally exempt. | [Financial Services Sector Guideline, section 4](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Financial-Services-Sector-Guideline.pdf) |
| VAT on bank fees is deductible with a tax invoice from the bank, or an alternative document agreed with ZATCA. | [Financial Services Sector Guideline, section 8.1](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Financial-Services-Sector-Guideline.pdf) |

## Imports and reverse charge

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Services received from a non-resident supplier are accounted for under the reverse charge mechanism (return field 9). | [Guideline on Imports and Exports, section 6.3](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-on-Imports-and-Exports-under-VAT-Provision.pdf) |
| VAT on imported goods is paid at customs and can be deducted (return field 8). | [Guideline on Imports and Exports, sections 4 and 10.3](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-on-Imports-and-Exports-under-VAT-Provision.pdf) |
| Exports are zero rated with export proof documents. | [Guideline on Imports and Exports, section 7](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-on-Imports-and-Exports-under-VAT-Provision.pdf) |

## Records

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Records are kept for at least six years from the end of the tax period. Capital assets and real estate have longer periods. | [Guideline: Tax Invoicing and Records, section 8.3.3](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-for-Tax-Invoicing-and-Records-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| Failure to keep tax invoices, books and records: a fine of up to SAR 50,000. | [ZATCA VAT penalties](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/VAT/Pages/Penalties.aspx) |

## How the KSA VAT Return report applies these rules

| Report behaviour | Rule it follows |
|---|---|
| Amounts are shown excluding VAT in the Amount and Adjustment columns, with the VAT in its own column. | [Simplified VAT filing guide](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Simplified_VAT_Filing_Guidelines.pdf) |
| Sales and purchases follow the form's sections, and field 13 is output VAT less input VAT. | [User guide: Submit VAT Return](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/eServices/Documents/eServices_Manual_009.pdf), [Simplified VAT filing guide](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Simplified_VAT_Filing_Guidelines.pdf) |
| Credit notes, debit notes and purchase returns are reported in the Adjustment column. | [Guideline: Tax Invoicing and Records, section 7](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-for-Tax-Invoicing-and-Records-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| Field 14 (corrections from previous periods) is labelled up to SAR ±15,000 and is filled by hand. | [Guideline: Tax Invoicing and Records, section 10.4](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-for-Tax-Invoicing-and-Records-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |
| Fields 8 and 9 (imports and reverse charge) are not calculated and must be completed from customs and non-resident supplier records. | [Guideline on Imports and Exports](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-on-Imports-and-Exports-under-VAT-Provision.pdf) |
| Opening entries and transfers between VAT accounts (settlement, payment, reclassification) are not reported: the return covers sales and purchases only. | [Simplified VAT filing guide](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Simplified_VAT_Filing_Guidelines.pdf) |
| The detail report lists every voucher behind an amount, with the voucher number, the supplier or customer invoice number, the party and its VAT number. Input VAT can be checked against valid tax invoices before filing. | [Guideline on Input Tax Deduction, section 8](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-on-Input-Tax-Deduction-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf) |

The report shows what is booked. Whether an input VAT amount is recoverable is decided by the rules above, so
review the [detail report](/reports/reports/ksa-vat-return-detail/) before filing.
