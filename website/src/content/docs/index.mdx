## ZATCA references and key obligations

<Aside type="tip" title="Built on official ZATCA rules">
	The VAT return report follows the published rules of the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority. Every obligation it
	is built on is listed with a link to its official source: registration, filing deadlines, corrections, tax
	invoices, e-invoicing, input VAT deduction, imports and records.
</Aside>

<LinkCard title="ZATCA obligations and references" description="The Saudi VAT obligations behind the VAT return report, each with its official ZATCA source." href="/reports/zatca/obligations/" />

## What the app does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Saudi VAT return" icon="document">
		Fields 1 to 16 of the return with amount, adjustment and VAT columns, laid out like the official form.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Every amount traceable" icon="magnifier">
		Click any amount to open the vouchers behind it: invoice numbers, parties, tax IDs, taxable amount and VAT.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Standard ERPNext reports" icon="approve-check">
		Script reports read submitted invoices and the general ledger. They never change your documents or entries.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Print, Excel and PDF" icon="download">
		A4 print views, and Excel or PDF export from the standard report menu.
	</Card>
	<Card title="No company data" icon="seti:lock">
		Accounts, rates and companies come from settings on your site. Nothing is hard-coded and tests use synthetic data.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Arabic and English" icon="translate">
		Every label, report and print in Arabic (right to left) and English.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
